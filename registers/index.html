    Coverage, matching and the CRUK navigation rank

    The current collection contains 42 England and Wales LRF areas and three Scottish Regional Resilience Partnership areas, with varying detail. Scotland is a preview of 26 narrative topics from the 2024 registers; these remain unscored, with reviewed links distinguishing direct scenarios from broader topic coverage. Some records are selected topics or summaries, not complete registers. An area without a matched entry may still face the selected risk.

    Direct links and reviewed broader topic links have distinct map colours. Use the checkbox to show direct matches only. Candidate and unreviewed broad links are excluded. Each Scottish link includes a rationale and local/national PDF page references. Review the local–national matches (CSV).

    CRUK uses a 1–5 navigation rank: Very Low = 1, Low = 2, Medium = 3, High = 4, Very High = 5. Otherwise, likelihood × impact products 1–5, 6–10, 11–15, 16–20 and 21–25 map to ranks 1–5; approximate graphical ratings are rounded. Missing scores stay unranked. These ranks are not official partnership scores or estimates of personal risk. Registers use different methods, so cross-area comparisons require caution.
