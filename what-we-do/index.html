WHAT WE DO

From knowing the risks to preparing together.

We’re bringing data, evidence, practical guidance and people together to help communities prepare.

Start with the risks

Local resilience partnerships (called Local Resilience Forums) publish assessments of what could happen, how likely it is and how serious the consequences could be. These Community Risk Registers can be difficult to find and compare. Our first prototypes bring those assessments together and keep links to the original sources.

  • By postcode finds the resilience partnership for your area.
  • By area lets you browse published local registers.
  • By risk shows where local assessments match a national risk.
  • Devon looks more closely at evidence on flooding, heat and wildfire fuel.

Make evidence easier to act on

Preparing might mean getting essential supplies together, protecting a bedroom from extreme heat or making a plan with neighbours. We want to connect people with practical guidance, trusted resources and organisations already doing this work.
