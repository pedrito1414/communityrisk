Read the evidence in context

Three useful views. No combined risk score.

The map window covers Devon and some neighbouring land and sea; it is not an administrative boundary. Postcode searches are limited to Devon, Plymouth and Torbay. Map clicks can explore the wider window. A postcode point can represent several properties, and local conditions may differ considerably.

Flood: detailed polygons, older service

The map and point query use the Environment Agency’s Risk of Flooding from Rivers and Sea service. Its published last data edit is 6 June 2024; this pilot does not claim to use the latest national flood assessment. The service accounts for flood defences. Flood map colours preserve its High, Medium, Low and Very Low labels. Map geometry is rounded for display; the point query is performed separately by the source service.

Zoom in to see polygons. An empty query means no polygon was returned by this service, not that flooding cannot happen. Surface water, groundwater, sewer flooding and future climate scenarios are not assessed here.

Heat: a historical summer, at regional scale

NASA POWER T2M_MAX, derived from MERRA-2, for 1 June–31 August 2022. The value shown is the highest daily maximum in that period. We also count days reaching 25°C, an illustrative threshold rather than an official heat-health definition. All 92 days must be present. Grid cells are 0.5° latitude × 0.625° longitude; neighbouring streets can share the same value. No interpolation or neighbourhood heat score is applied. This is not HadUK-Grid, a future projection, indoor overheating, or a current forecast.

Data obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project, funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science Program. Data documentation · Reuse guidance, including commercial use · Download sample and exact API URLs.

Wildfire: fuel context, not a model of risk

A stored EFFIS fuel_map WMS image, described by its capabilities as “Fuel Map - ver. 2017”, and its original legend. The image is rendered to fit the pilot window; its pixels do not establish the underlying analytical resolution. The service does not state an analytical resolution for this layer. Fuel type alone omits weather, moisture, ignition, terrain and suppression. Uncoloured areas must not be read as no wildfire risk.
