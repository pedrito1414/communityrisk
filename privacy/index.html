About this website

Privacy and visit statistics

We use GoatCounter to understand how often the tools are used and which features are helpful. Our integration does not set tracking cookies or add a persistent visitor ID.

We send the page visited, the referring website’s domain, screen width, and counts of actions such as completing a postcode lookup, choosing a forum, opening a risk, following a source link, or downloading the local–national matches. Area and risk codes may identify the selected regional records.

We do not send full postcodes, postcode coordinates, text entered into searches, or query strings and fragments from page addresses to GoatCounter. Like other web services, GoatCounter receives the visitor’s IP address and browser information when a request reaches its servers. Its privacy policy explains how it processes that information.

Your choice

We respect the browser’s Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can also exclude your visits below. This choice is stored only in this browser; clearing site storage removes the saved choice. It does not delete statistics already collected.

Postcode lookups and maps

A postcode you submit is sent to Postcodes.io to find its location. New shareable addresses contain the selected Local Resilience Forum, without the postcode or its coordinates. Old postcode links prefill the form and require submission before a lookup; the postcode is removed from the explorer’s address once the form is ready. The postcode marker is temporary and is not restored from a shared link. Background map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, and the mapping library loads from unpkg. These services receive connection information when your browser contacts them.

Local previews do not send visit statistics. Analytics blockers may also prevent counting. No GoatCounter login is required to use the website.
