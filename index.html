02 / ABOUT CLIMATE RESILIENT UK

It started with a question in Exeter.

“How do I know what risks I should actually be preparing for?”

Much of the necessary information and data exists, but it is scattered across government reports, risk registers and research. Practical guidance on preparing for emergencies and recovering from them is similarly hard to find.

Read our story →

03 / WHAT WE DO

From knowing the risks to preparing together.

We’re starting by making published risk registers easier to find, understand and explore. We want to connect that evidence with practical guidance, trusted resources and the people already working to make their communities safer.

We’re open to working with partners on community resilience.
