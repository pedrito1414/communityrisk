Our goal

Make local risk information easier to find and understand.

Local Resilience Forums in England and Wales and Regional Resilience Partnerships in Scotland bring emergency responders and other organisations together to prepare for emergencies. Their Community Risk Registers describe risks such as flooding, severe weather, disease, and disruption to essential services.

Climate Resilient UK aims to bring these separate publications together, preserve links to the original evidence, and offer different ways to explore the same information. Choose the starting point that works for you.

Methodology and limitations

What the collection covers

The collection represents 42 Local Resilience Forum areas in England and Wales and three Regional Resilience Partnership areas in Scotland. Detail varies: some records come from scored tables, others from selected headlines or narrative summaries. Scotland currently contains 26 unscored topics from the North, East and West 2024 registers. Northern Ireland is not yet included.

How local and national risks are linked

Explore by risk connects local entries to scenarios in the UK National Risk Register 2026. Green indicates a direct scenario match. Ochre with a dashed outline indicates reviewed broader topic coverage: a local topic relates to a narrower national risk, but does not establish that its exact scale, location or assumptions were assessed locally. One local topic can link to several national scenarios.

Reviewed broader links are included by default; untick “Include reviewed broader topic coverage” to show direct matches only. Candidate and unreviewed broad matches are excluded. The Scottish links include our editorial rationale and source-page references; they are not classifications endorsed by the register publishers. Existing England and Wales links remain provisional. An area without a highlighted entry may still face the risk.

What the ratings mean

Published risk labels map to a 1–5 navigation rank. Where a recognised label is absent, we use published likelihood × impact bands, then an approximate graphical rating if available. Missing ratings remain unranked. Linking to a national scenario never supplies a missing local score. These navigation ranks are not official scores, probabilities or directly comparable measures of personal risk: registers differ in scope, dates and methods.

Geography, sources and uncertainty

The map shows resilience partnership areas, not the locations or extent of individual hazards. England and Wales use stored ONS-based boundaries labelled December 2024; Scotland uses Scottish Government boundaries distributed as the 2019 shapefile, repaired and simplified for web display. Postcodes locate an approximate point within these boundaries; results near coasts or boundaries can be ambiguous. These regional assessments do not predict risk to a property or individual.

Plain-English definitions retain links to their sources. Where an exact local definition is unavailable, national summaries or related background may be shown with a separate label. Sources are maintained snapshots, not live feeds. This is not a complete, independently audited transcription of every register; source dates, extracted text and provisional matches require continued checking. Always consult the original publication for its full assessment.
