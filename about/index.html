ABOUT CLIMATE RESILIENT UK

It started with a question in Exeter.

How do I know what risks I should actually be preparing for?

Why we started

Floods, heatwaves and storms are already disrupting lives and livelihoods across the UK. Reading David Shukman’s The Response brought home how little preparation had taken place at every level, from government to individual households.

At a gathering on emergency preparedness in Exeter, people wanted to know which risks mattered most for Exeter and Devon. Much of the necessary information and data exists, but it is scattered across government reports, risk registers and research. Practical guidance on preparing for emergencies and recovering from them is similarly hard to find.

What we’re working towards

Climate Resilient UK brings together data, evidence and practical knowledge so people can understand local risks, find actions they can take and connect with others doing the same.

Neighbours, community groups, local organisations and public authorities can do more when they work together. As the evidence changes, we want to make it easier for people to learn from each other and share what works.
